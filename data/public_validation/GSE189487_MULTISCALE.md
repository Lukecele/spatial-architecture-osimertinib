# GSE189487 multiscale lung audit

The common spatial-context skeleton was evaluated at 55, 100, 150, 200, 300
and 500 µm using the lung-specific representation. The analysis is
[`src/validate_gse189487_multiscale.py`](../../src/validate_gse189487_multiscale.py);
the output is [`GSE189487_multiscale.json`](./GSE189487_multiscale.json).

The radii are a sensitivity grid, not six independent confirmatory tests. The
best radius in this same cohort cannot be presented as independently validated.
Patient/sample-level effects and their variability are retained for the next
independent cohort.
