Subcellular Spatial Architecture (Visium HD 2 µm)

10x Visium HD Subcellular Spatial Architecture and Microvascular Decoupling in Osimertinib Resistance (λ ≈ 624 µm)

Luca Celebrano • School of Medicine and Surgery, Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II & TIGEM

Study Overview

Subcellular investigation across 211,104 single-cell bins at 2 µm resolution: demonstrating that homotypic neoplastic clustering collapses functional capillary contact from 24.8% to 2.7%, triggering interstitial fluid pressure barriers that exceed TKI diffusion limits.

Explore GitHub Repository Master Study Portal

2 µm Resolution

Utilizes 10x Genomics Visium HD with 211,104 individual spatial bins to map subcellular micro-neighborhoods in intact human biopsies.

Microvascular Collapse

Quantifies dramatic reduction in endothelial vascular contact from 24.8% in coupled tissue down to 2.7% in solid/micropapillary nests (p < 10^-16).

Reaction-Diffusion Barrier

Proves that small-molecule osimertinib penetration length scale lambda ≈ 624 µm fails to sterilize hypoxic cores in nests exceeding 200 µm radius.

Viable Sanctuary Window

Defines the precise spatial annular niche (100–200 µm from vessels) where tumor cells remain viable but receive sub-therapeutic drug levels.
